### Current Obstacles

**False Alarms**
- Sensor inaccuracies due to humidity, temperature, interference

**Cybersecurity Risks**
- IoT vulnerabilities and potential data breaches

**Cost**
- Initial setup: $200–$1,000+ per unit

**Maintenance**
- Periodic calibration and battery replacement required

**Regulatory Compliance**
- UL and IEC certification requirements
