### Core Components

**Sensors**
- Gas detectors (methane, propane, CO, CO₂)
- Technologies: electrochemical, infrared, catalytic

**Connectivity**
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, cellular networks

**Central Hub/Cloud Platform**
- Data processing and storage
- Remote access via apps/dashboards

**Alerts & Actuators**
- Alarms, automatic shut-off valves, ventilation triggers
