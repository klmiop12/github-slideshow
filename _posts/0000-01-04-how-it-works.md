### Process Flow

1. **Continuous Monitoring**
   - Sensors sample air quality 24/7

2. **Data Transmission**
   - Information sent to hub or cloud

3. **Analysis**
   - Algorithms detect anomalies

4. **Response**
   - Trigger alerts (SMS, app notifications)
   - Activate safety measures (valve closure, ventilation)
